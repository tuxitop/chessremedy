# Move Accuracy Research

## Question

How should ChessRemedy measure player move accuracy?

## Investigate

- Stockfish centipawn loss
- Average centipawn loss
- WDL-based metrics
- Expected score
- Existing Lichess methodology
- Existing Chess.com methodology where publicly documented
- Position-dependent accuracy
- Winning/losing position effects
- Time-control effects

## Required output

Determine a defensible ChessRemedy accuracy methodology.

## Important

Do not assume that a raw centipawn-loss threshold is sufficient.
