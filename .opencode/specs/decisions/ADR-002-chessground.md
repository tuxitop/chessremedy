# ADR-002: Chessboard Library

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Use:

`@lichess-org/chessground@10.1.1 or a higher version`

## Reasons

ChessRemedy requires:

- responsive board interaction
- arrows
- highlights
- touch support
- orientation
- board interaction suitable for training

## Constraint

Do not replace the library without a new ADR.
